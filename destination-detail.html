Thanjavur – The Land of Temples

4.8 (240+ Traveler Reviews)

Thanjavur, also known as Tanjore, is famous for its rich cultural heritage, magnificent temples, and the grandeur of Chola architecture. Recognized worldwide as a center of Carnatic music, bronze idol casting, and Tanjore paintings, this historic city holds the immortal legacy of King Raja Raja Chola I.

Top Attractions

  • Brihadeeswarar Temple (Big Temple - UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Thanjavur Royal Palace & Art Gallery
  • Saraswathi Mahal Library (Ancient Palm Leaf Manuscripts)
  • Schwartz Church & Sivaganga Park
